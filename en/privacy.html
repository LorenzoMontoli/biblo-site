Privacy policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Draft pending review. This text describes faithfully how the application works, but it is not legal advice: it must be reviewed by a professional before distribution, and the fields in square brackets must be completed.

In short

Biblo is an application that runs on your computer. The documents you index and the search store stay there: we don't receive them, don't store them, don't see them. The only moment anything leaves your computer is the chat, and what leaves is just the passage of text needed to answer the question you have just asked.

This website uses no profiling cookies and no third-party analytics.

1. Who processes the data

Data controller: [NAME / LEGAL ENTITY], [ADDRESS], reachable at [CONTACT EMAIL].

If you use your own key with the language model provider, for those requests you are the controller: they go through your account and your contractual relationship with the provider.

2. How the application works

Indexing happens entirely on your device: files and the search index are never uploaded anywhere. When you ask a question, the application sends the language model provider your question and the most relevant passages retrieved from your documents, which are what the answer is built from. Nothing else leaves the device, and only for that request.

3. What data is processed

  • Document excerpts and conversation text — sent to the model provider to generate the answer. These may contain personal data if your documents do.
  • Application settings — assistant name, language, keys, folder paths: stored locally only.
  • Conversation history — stored on your device only.
  • The site's download count — an aggregate count of started downloads, with no personal data and no identifiers.

4. The language model provider

Answers are generated through the Cerebras API (api.cerebras.ai). Processing of the data sent to that API is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy, which we encourage you to read: [TO VERIFY AND QUOTE HERE: the clause on data submitted via the API and its use for training].

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The model provider may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where this happens under our responsibility, the transfer relies on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR [standard contractual clauses and/or applicable derogations, to be finalised]. If you use your own key, the transfer takes place within your relationship with the provider.

6. Legal basis

Where we are the controller, processing is based on performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — delivering the answer you asked for — and on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in operating and securing the service. You choose which documents to submit: avoid special categories of data unless you have a legal basis for it.

7. How long

Excerpts and questions are transmitted for the duration of a single request and are not retained by us. Conversations and settings stay on your device until you delete them. The download count is an aggregate number and cannot be traced to a person.

8. Your rights

You may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (Arts. 15-22 GDPR) and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. Since almost all data stays on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly from the application. For anything else, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we increment its version and the application asks you to read and accept it again.
